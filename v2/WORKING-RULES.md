# V2 Working Rules

## Booking
- Minimum lead time: 2 hours.
- Services starting before 12:00 must be fully confirmed/paid by 21:00 the previous day.
- Artist response: 15 minutes when service is 2–4 hours away; otherwise normally 2 hours.
- Requests received after 21:00 may allow response until next day 12:00, but never later than payment deadline minus 15 minutes.
- No-response request may be resent once.
- On resend, existing text/options remain populated and the customer may edit them before sending.

## Sessions
- Phone + OTP.
- One account = one active session.
- Successful OTP login on another device revokes the previous session.

## Payout
- Artist completion button is removed.
- Customer confirmation activates after scheduled service end.
- Auto-payout eligibility starts 6 hours after service end only when delivery is confirmed and there is no dispute/cancellation/admin hold.
- If due time is 00:00–09:00, postpone to 12:00 that calendar day.

## Artist identity/payment details
- Artist can edit profile/content.
- Legal identity, registration number, bank, bank account/IBAN and account-holder identity are editable until verified.
- After registration + bank/account verification succeeds, those verified fields become locked.
- Public/profile fields remain editable.
